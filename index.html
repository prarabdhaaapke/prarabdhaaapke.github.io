Questions

What does Prarabdha mean?

Prarabdha comes from Sanskrit and means "begun" or "commenced". In the karma teachings it is the portion of past action that has already begun to bear fruit in this life. Our approach: you cannot change the weather, but you can dress for it.

Do you predict bad events?

No. We never predict death, accidents, illness or anything frightening. Readings describe tendencies and good times to act, in gentle language, for reflection.

Will you tell me if an ex will return?

The free reunion reading checks your 5th, 7th and 11th houses, Venus, retrograde planets, Rahu and Ketu and your running dasha. It gives you a reunion score, the best windows to reconnect and honest guidance. It describes your chart, not the other person's choice.
